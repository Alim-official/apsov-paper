## Abstract

The paper considers the degradation of the first wall of a fusion reactor under the combined action of helium fluxes, hydrogen isotopes, and fast neutrons. A multilayer protective coating architecture W/h-BN/graphene is proposed. Modeling was performed by molecular dynamics with NEP89 potential in GPUMD. Validation: tungsten lattice parameter deviation 0%, C–C bond 0.02%, B–N bond 0.06%. The three-layer system is stable at 1200 K. Adhesion energy W/h-BN is −16.48 eV. NEP89 is unsuitable for helium — a specialized potential is required.
